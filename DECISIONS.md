# Decision log

This file records every methodological and engineering decision. "Decided by"
gives the person who made the decision. Decisions that affect results are
made by Salvatore Barbagallo (SB), the analyst. Claude Code (CC) makes only
decisions that do not change results, and they are labelled as such.

| D-016 | 2026-10-23 | Survival extrapolation | Base-case OS: lognormal, fitted separately to each arm, with the general-population mortality floor applied. Scenarios: log-logistic, generalised gamma, RP spline (1 knot), Gompertz, Weibull, exponential, and the TA531 piecewise approach (23, 33, 43 weeks). | CC (delegated by SB) | Lowest combined AIC across arms (ΔAIC 0.8 pembro, 0.0 chemo) and best BIC in chemo. Reproduces observed 5-year OS (31.5% vs 31.6%; 16.0% vs 16.4%). Its falling hazard matches the smoothed empirical hazard. The generalised gamma's Q includes 0 in both arms (pembro 0.39, 95% CI −0.27 to 1.05; chemo −0.07, −0.68 to 0.54), so lognormal is not rejected. Its 10-year projections sit in the middle of the well-fitting models. Gompertz was rejected for its implausible plateau, and Weibull and exponential for poor fit. Same family in both arms, as TSD 14 advises; the OS proportional-hazards test does not reject PH (p = 0.66). | Yes |
| D-017 | 2026-10-23 | Survival extrapolation | Base-case PFS: lognormal, fitted separately to each arm, capped at OS. | D-018 | 2026-10-23 | Model structure | Three-state partitioned survival model (progression-free, progressed, dead); weekly cycles; horizon 20 years (1,044 weekly cycles); NHS and PSS perspective; costs and QALYs discounted at 3.5% per year. Half-cycle correction uses the trapezoidal (life-table) method: occupancy in a cycle is the mean of state membership at its start and end. Discount factors are continuous-time, evaluated at each cycle's midpoint: (1 + r)^−t, with t in years. One-off event costs (death, progression) fall in the cycle they occur and are discounted at its midpoint. | CC (delegated by SB) | Matches TA531's structure (weekly cycle with half-cycle correction, source checks C3–C4; 20-year horizon, N1) and the NICE manual §4.5.1 (3.5%). The trapezoidal method is the most accurate standard correction for a cohort trace. | Yes |
| D-019 | 2026-10-23 | Utilities | Base case: TA531 time-to-death utilities, pooled across arms (committee papers Table 62). Each arm's utility per cycle is the mean over the four time-to-death bands (<30, 30–180, 180–360, ≥360 days), weighted by the probability of death in each band implied by that arm's OS curve. The ≥360-day value is capped at the general-population EQ-5D norm for the cohort's age and sex (the committee's preference, TA531 §3.14). All utilities are age-adjusted multiplicatively with the NICE DSU 2022 EQ-5D-3L norms. Scenario: progression-based utilities (Table 63: PF 0.778, PD 0.668), age-adjusted. No separate AE disutility, because the time-to-death utilities come from trial EQ-5D that already reflects AEs. | CC (delegated by SB) | The brief makes time-to-death the main approach when TA531 values are public, and they are. DSU 2022 norms are NICE's current recommended source for age adjustment. | Yes |
